# Hints

## General

- The [lists tutorial](https://mlochbaum.github.io/BQN/tutorial/list.html) introduces list notation and
  arithmetic on lists.
- The [range documentation](https://mlochbaum.github.io/BQN/doc/range.html) describes `↕`.

## 1. Convert steps to metres

- Arithmetic on a list works on every element: no loop is needed.

## 2. Check the daily goal

- Comparisons work on lists too.

## 3. Count the days

- One primitive gives the length of a list.

## 4. Record a new day

- `∾` joins lists, and a single number counts as a list of one.

## 5. Compare two weeks

- Two lists of the same length combine position by position.

## 6. Number the days

- `↕` counts from zero; you need as many numbers as there are days.
